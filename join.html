Vetted 1099s for overflow Pinellas work

Knight Group Handyman Services LLC is a registered, insured Safety Harbor company. When the board is full, we use independent contractors for handyman-scope overflow — drywall, paint, carpentry, punch lists, hardware, and finish closeout.

This is 1099 contractor work, not W-2 employment. Applying does not create a job offer. We review documents, confirm insurance, and only then talk about fit and availability.

Knight Group is not a licensed electrical, plumbing, or HVAC contractor. Do not apply expecting to perform potable-water connections, service-panel work, or other licensed-trade work as Knight Group. Those jobs are routed to licensed providers.

FAQ

Contractor questions

Straight answers before you send documents.

Is this a W-2 job with Knight Group?

No. This page is for independent contractors (1099). Knight Group does not hire W-2 employees through this form.

What does Knight Group pay 1099 contractors?

Contractor pay is variable and is not the homeowner rate. For general handyman jobs, typical contractor pay is $75 for the first hour of that job and $50 for each hour after on the same job. Specialty, vendor, and unusual scopes can pay differently. We confirm the ticket before you take the work. Homeowners use published customer pricing.

What documents do I need to apply?

A completed W-9, a current general liability certificate of insurance, and either a workers’ compensation policy or a Florida Division of Workers’ Compensation exemption. List Knight Group Handyman Services LLC, 1225 7th St S, Safety Harbor, FL 34695 as the certificate holder on the liability COI.

Does applying mean Knight Group is hiring licensed plumbers or electricians as the prime contractor?

No. Knight Group is a registered and insured handyman company, not a licensed electrical, plumbing, or HVAC contractor. Overflow work is handyman-scope. Licensed-trade connections are routed to appropriately licensed providers. See Florida handyman scope.

I am a homeowner. Is this the right page?

No. Use booking for a written estimate, or contact for a shorter question. This form is only for contractors applying to subcontract.
