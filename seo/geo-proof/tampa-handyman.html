One November visit covered a full list at a Tampa home. Kitchen ceiling water stain and drywall patch

A Tampa kitchen ceiling had a water stain and failing tape. Sergey cut out a 10 by 12 inch section, patched it with new drywall, removed the loose coating and tape, re-taped and re-coated, matched the texture, sealed the water spots with an oil-based primer, and painted the damaged section. See the kitchen ceiling before and after.

Roof vent leak and master bath ceiling repair

Water was getting in around a roof vent and showing up in the master bath ceiling. Gavin raised and reset the vent, cut off the damaged section, installed a new rain hood, and sealed the openings with roofing sealant. Inside, he cut out the wet ceiling, hung new drywall, and added knockdown texture to match the rest of the room. See the roof vent work and the bath ceiling repair.

Drying equipment after a sink overflow

After a sink was left running in a Tampa home and a cleaning crew removed the standing water, Nick set a dehumidifier and an air mover so the rooms could keep drying before any repairs. See the drying setup.
