How a Clearwater job works

  1. Send details and photos. Use the quote form or call (813) 649-3341. Tell us the address, what needs fixing, and any access or condo building rules.
  2. Get a free written quote. We quote from your description and photos. We do not do free in-person estimate visits.
  3. Approve and schedule. You sign the agreement before work starts, and then we set a time.
  4. Work and photos. The tech does the agreed scope and photographs before and after. If the scope turns out different once we are on site, we revise the quote with you before doing extra work.
