How a Pinellas County job works

  1. Send details and photos. Use the quote form or call (813) 649-3341 with the city, what needs fixing, and a wide shot plus a close-up.
  2. Get a free written quote. We quote in writing from your photos and details ($150 first hour / $75 each additional hour standard; $200 / $100 specialty; no two-hour minimum). We do not do free in-person estimate visits.
  3. Approve and schedule. Work starts after you sign the written estimate, on a standard arrival window (8–10, 10–12, or 12–2) along our Pinellas routes.
  4. Work and photo closeout. The tech completes the agreed scope and photographs before and after. Any scope change on site is revised with you in writing before extra work starts.
