How the caulking repair was handled

From the truck, caulking repair in Gulfport, Pinellas County is a sequence: stabilize, correct structure or hardware, then prove the close with photos.

  1. Remove the failed joint

    Cut and scrape old material instead of laying a new bead on top of a dirty bond.

  2. Clean and dry

    Solvent or soap cleanup, then dry time so the new sealant can wet the surface.

  3. Tool a continuous bead

    Match the joint size to the gap, not a decorative smear.

  4. Cure before use

    Keep the area dry for the manufacturer window listed on the tube.
