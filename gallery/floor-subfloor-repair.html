Subfloor and floor repair scope

Soft spots, squeaks, and tile cracks often trace to subfloor damage from prior leaks or pest activity. This project addressed degraded decking before new floor covering could be installed — preventing a repeat failure months later.

  • Finished flooring removed only where necessary to expose damage
  • Rotten or delaminated subfloor sections cut back to solid structure
  • New plywood screwed on proper joist layout with gap allowance
  • Transition planning for the replacement floor material
  • Site left broom-clean with fasteners and debris removed
