How the fence repair was handled

If you live in Pinellas County and you are searching because the problem is in your way every day, this fence repair write-up is meant to read like the visit you actually want, not a brochure.

  1. Document the failed run

    Photograph the lean, the post bases, and any gate hardware before anything is pulled apart so the owner can see why the section moved.

  2. Relieve the load

    Support or unfasten racked panels so remaining posts are not forced further out of plumb while the failed members come out.

  3. Reset structure

    Replace or reset posts that have lost bearing, then rebuild rails so pickets can land on a straight, fastened line.

  4. Reattach the skin

    Refasten pickets or panels to the corrected frame, matching spacing where the existing run is still sound.

  5. Prove the close

    Check plumb, latch, and neighbor-side appearance, then leave before-and-after photos on the ticket.
